A commando skis downhill between trees, enemy skiers and a flying machine that drops things from the air: the course and its four stretches of snow, the bonus and the title from the ROM's bytes, checked dot by dot against openMSX; the game, the jump, the bends, the flying machine, the crash and the bonus played in GIF, and the six tunes from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1984 - 16 KB cartridge at 0x8000 - MSX - Dump 17d6db13...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The logo and the screens come from running the ROM (checked against openMSX), and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the skier, the shot, the jump, the controls, the points and the lives, as the code has them.
  • The course: the screen from the ROM, its four stretches of snow, the bends, the trees and rocks, and the six difficulties.
  • The enemies: the enemy skiers, the flying machine and what it drops, played in GIF.
  • The sequences: the title, the demo, PATTERN CLEAR, the bonus, the crash and GAME OVER.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the six tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: the jump into the middle of the BIOS, the copies that run over, the font without an O, the orders that do not exist and the frames that run late.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the BIOS it uses, the interrupt, the RAM, the sound engine and the tools.
